# Credits

All art and sound in this mod was generated for it (no files taken from other games):
- Sprites (Fallen One, Skeleton, Hell Bovine, Butcher, health and mana globes, gold, firebolt, sorcerer hand): generated with Nano Banana 2 through fal.ai, then cut out and resized with the kit scripts.
- Floor and wall textures: generated with Z-Image Turbo (tiling) through fal.ai.
- Sound effects: generated with ElevenLabs Sound Effects v2; the Butcher's voice line with ElevenLabs v3 text to speech, both through fal.ai.
- Code: original ZScript written for this mod.
- Runs on GZDoom with the Freedoom: Phase 2 game data (BSD licensed).
